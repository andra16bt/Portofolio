## Design approach

The site follows a **neo-brutalism** style: thick black borders, hard offset shadows, flat bright colors, and big expressive type (Archivo Black for headings, Space Grotesk for text, Caveat for handwritten accents). I wanted the portfolio to feel playful and confident, a bit like a sticker book, while staying easy to read and easy to navigate.

Small details I enjoyed building:

- An entry screen you open by pressing the up arrow or swiping up
- A slow-moving grid background with floating shapes
- Cards and tiles that pop in with a slight tilt as you scroll
- A photo gallery that keeps every picture in its original proportions
